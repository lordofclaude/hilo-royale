How you survive

The match plays. The feed rolls. Roughly every ten minutes of match time, the arena asks one question with a hard lock — and the whole lobby answers at once.

Read the match

Real TxLINE score events drive every round: corners, shots, cards, VAR reviews, halftime subs. No trivia, no fluff — if it didn't happen in the fixture, it isn't a question.

Call it before the lock

Six seconds. The crowd bar shows the lobby splitting live under your cursor. Commit HI or LO — or the timer commits for you.

Outlive 99 rivals

Your 99 rivals are clearly labeled simulated bots — skill and picks are deterministically seeded from one fulfilled ORAO randomness source on Solana devnet. Every wrong call is an elimination you watch: a named dot going dark. Per-lobby seed commitments are next.

Send your ghost

Finish a run and share a ghost challenge link — a friend faces the same fixture and the same questions with your picks replaying against theirs. Crowns are settled head to head.
