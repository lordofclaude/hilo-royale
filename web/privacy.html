PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE.

Prototype status

Hi-Lo Royale is a July 2026 hackathon prototype. It does not take wagers, sell personal data, or run third-party advertising analytics.

Website

A guest handle, ladder score, settings, and completed-run details are stored locally in your browser. They are not uploaded by the replay game. Clear the site's storage in your browser to delete them.

iOS app

Guest or Google sign-in identity, profile progress, and settings are stored on your device. If the optional room-presence service is configured, the app sends the display identity needed to show presence in that lobby. Use Delete local data in Settings to remove Hi-Lo data from the device.

Match and infrastructure data

Match events and odds come from TxODDS TxLINE. Proof links open Solscan. Hosting and upstream providers may process standard network metadata such as IP address and request time to deliver and protect their services.

Retention and sharing

Replay progress stays local unless you choose Share, Copy challenge, or join an optional room. A challenge URL includes the fixture, recorded picks, and public fan handle so another fan can race the run. Do not use a handle that reveals sensitive information.

Contact

For the hackathon build, use the repository contact supplied with the submission. This notice will be replaced with production legal terms before any public launch.
